# Changelog

## 2023

### Early market signal
- Noted that B2B growth stacks were getting better at capture than at routing.
- Observed repeated speed-to-lead failures caused by owner conflict, enrichment lag, and nurture misclassification.

## 2024

### Design framing
- Defined the repo around routing integrity rather than generic CRM administration.
- Focused the operating model on route reason, latency, ownership pressure, and next action.

## 2025

### Prototype
- Modeled high-fit enterprise, partner, and self-serve lanes.
- Added policy-style bottleneck framing to distinguish data defects from routing defects.

## 2026-05-17

### v0.1
- Shipped the first public control surface.
- Added queue, routing-rules, bottlenecks, verification, and docs routes.
- Added real browser-rendered proof assets for the README.

### v1.0
- Published as part of the Growth / Analytics / Traffic Integrity portfolio shift.
- Positioned as the first GTM systems repo in the next build wave.
